# Mellu.mate hero concepts

All four use the same original mascot: a small, round, cream-white helper shaped like a soft eraser, with a mint leaf on top. The illustrations have no text. The name goes on top as real text later.

1. `concept-1.webp`, sticky-note board. The mascot pins notes onto a cork board, with empty cream space on the left.
   Busy and helpful, the clearest "keeps your project tidy" story. The left space fits the wordmark well, but the mascot sits near the middle and a stray speck floats above it.
2. `concept-2.webp`, notebook page. The mascot writes in an open notebook, with sparkles around it.
   The calmest and warmest of the four, and the pencil reads as "working on your plan". Wide and low, so it suits a hero, but the empty space is at the top, not at the side.
3. `concept-3.webp`, desk with stationery. The mascot stands on a tidy shelf between a plant, books and a pencil cup.
   Strong "roommate" feeling, with a large empty wall for the name. The mascot is small, so at README width it may read as a still life more than a character.
4. `concept-4.webp`, sticker sheet. The mascot in five sticker poses, with small stamp dots and a tape roll.
   The most playful, and the best source for the square logo. As a hero it is busy, and it has no clear space for the name.

## Round 2: stationery ring and balloon letters

Flat sticker style, thick brown outlines, pastel colors, notebook grid paper. Made with Ideogram v3 on fal, which can draw text.

5. `concept-5.webp`, the name with smiling stationery around it. The name reads correctly, in capitals on two lines.
   The cutest of round 2, and the faces give it the "friendly helper" feeling. The ring is loose, and the left edge has notebook holes.
6. `concept-6.webp`, the name with plain stationery, no faces. Capitals on two lines, with a small heart for the dot.
   The closest to your sticker-pack reference, and the most "tidy desk". The ruler carries a few garbled letters, and the dot between the words is lost.
7. `concept-7.webp`, the name and smiling stationery on one big sticker, on grid paper with sticky notes in the corners.
   Reads as one badge, so it also works cropped square for the logo. The objects are small, so the ring idea is weaker.
8. `concept-8.webp`, the stationery ring with an empty middle, and no text.
   The name goes in the middle as real text drawn in code, in "Mellu.mate" mixed case with the dot. That is the only way to get the exact spelling, and the text stays sharp at any size.

## Round 3: drawn in code

Every object and letter is drawn by `docs/art/draw.py`, so each object has its real shape and the name is spelled exactly, on one line. Pastel colors only, and a white sticker edge with a soft shadow.

9. `concept-9.webp` and `concept-9-logo.webp`, twelve stationery stickers in a ring around "Mellu.mate", five of them with small faces.
   Simple and modern, and the name reads clearly at any size. The faces add a little warmth without a mascot.
10. `concept-10.webp` and `concept-10-logo.webp`, the same without faces.
   The calmest and most modern version. It is a little less "friendly helper".

## Chosen: concept 9

The README uses concept 9. `docs/art/draw.py` draws it and `docs/art/render.sh` renders it into `docs/assets/`: the hero in light and dark (`hero-light.png`, `hero-dark.png`, 1640x820, shown at 820 wide), the divider (`divider-light.png`, `divider-dark.png`), and the square logo (`logo.png`, 512x512) for the GitHub avatar and social preview.
